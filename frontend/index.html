About this data

Detailed checks cover the last 30 days. The outage archive includes every retained incident lasting more than a minute, plus missing observations within the detailed check window.

Missing observations count as system downtime after the three-minute freshness deadline. Overlapping failed checks and missing observations are combined into a single outage for each service.
